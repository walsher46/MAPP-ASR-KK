# MAPP-ASR-KK
A morphology-aware post-processing (MAPP) ASR neuro-symbolic framework for Kazakh that integrates rule-based normalization, morphological segmentation, phonetic correction, and semantic ranking. The approach was evaluated on a dataset of 100 Kazakh audio recordings of varying lengths and domains. Experimental results demonstrate substantial improvements across all evaluation metrics: WER decreased from 73.18% to 31.16% (−42.02 pp), CER from 33.67% to 17.68%, and TER from 74.94% to 34.88%. Semantic and n-gram metrics also improved significantly, with BLEU increasing from 7.93 to 48.86 and BERTScore from 0.781 to 0.908. These findings confirm that morphology-aware post-processing is an effective and practical approach for improving ASR performance in low-resource agglutinative languages without retraining acoustic models
